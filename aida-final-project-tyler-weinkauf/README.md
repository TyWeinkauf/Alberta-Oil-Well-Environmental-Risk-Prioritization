## Shared project milestones and progress evidence

The checkpoints below are feedback opportunities, not extra grades. Progress is judged by the evidence you can show against your course’s project requirements: whether the scope is clear, the inputs are permitted and understood, the workflow runs, checks reveal limitations, claims match results, and another person can follow the final package. The official course rubric remains the grading measure.

| Week | Evidence to bring | What you should be able to explain |
|---|---|---|
| 6 — scope and feasibility | Approved proposal and any feedback; question; user and decision; course-by-course scope matrix; data source and permission; a small check that your data and plan will work. | What problem are you addressing, what is included, and is it possible with permitted data and course tools? |
| 8 — design | Data inventory/sample; provenance; workflow or analysis plan; baseline; quality/success checks. | What will you do to the data, what will the output look like, and how will you know it is valid? |
| 10 — prototype | A small end-to-end run from input to first output, with a reproducible command or clear QGIS/BI steps and a run note. | Can you repeat the workflow and show one result? |
| 12 — validation and revision | Tests, metrics or spatial checks; sensitivity/changed setting; error or failure example; limitation; changes made. | What does the evidence support, what does it not support, and what did you change after checking? |
| 14 — release candidate | Organized folders; current README/scope matrix; clean-run and privacy checks; peer feedback and your response; presentation outline. | Can another person locate, inspect, and understand the work and its limits? |
| 15 — final handoff | Clean final package(s); manifest; D2L submission; final presentation and reflection. | Can you demonstrate the working project, trace a result to evidence, explain a limitation, and defend your next step? |

Use `project-progress.md` to record status (Not started, In progress, Ready for feedback, Feedback received, Complete), evidence location, and next action. Do not change a milestone to Complete until the named evidence is present.
