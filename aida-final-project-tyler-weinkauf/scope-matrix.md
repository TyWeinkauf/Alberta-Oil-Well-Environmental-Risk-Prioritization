## Shared project context (if applicable)

- **Working project title:** [Title] -Alberta Oil Well Environmental Screening and Prioritization Tool
- **Shared practical problem:** [One or two sentences] - Environmental reclamation companies may need to review large numbers of oil and gas well sites and identify which sites warrant closer environmental review. This training project will demonstrate how permitted public well and environmental data can be prepared, spatially analyzed, and presented through an interactive mapping application to help prioritize further review.
- **Project boundary:** [Study area/population, time period, unit, or system included] - The project will examine permitted public oil and gas well records within a defined Alberta study area and selected environmental factors for which suitable public spatial data can be obtained. The final geographic extent, data period, environmental factors, and screening thresholds will be established after data availability and quality are confirmed.
- **What is outside scope:** [What you will not build or claim] - The project will not determine whether a well is environmentally unsafe, predict spills or environmental damage, determine regulatory compliance, or provide professional engineering or environmental recommendations. It will not replace field inspections, environmental assessments, regulatory review, consultation, or professional judgment. The application and its results are for educational and training purposes only and must not be used for real-world operational or environmental decisions.
- **Shared inputs:** [Files/data used by more than one course, with their source and permission] - Permitted public Alberta oil and gas well location and attribute data and selected permitted public environmental/geographic datasets may be used by both courses. OpenStreetMap will be used as the base map for the interactive web application. Each input's source, permission/licence, date/version, relevant fields, spatial reference information, and known limitations will be documented.
- **Integration point:** [How the separate course results connect, without treating one course result as proof for another] - AIDA 1145 will produce a cleaned, validated, documented, analysis-ready dataset from the permitted source data. AIDA 2362 will use appropriate spatial information from the prepared data to calculate and communicate environmental screening results through an interactive web map. The GIS component will include spatial validation, a baseline screening method, and sensitivity testing. Although the courses share inputs and a practical context, the Data Engineering pipeline and GIS spatial analysis will maintain separate methods, validation evidence, outputs, and assessment requirements.
- **Completion by end of Week 15:** [What can be finished and shown by December 7, 2026] - A reproducible data pipeline will produce the documented analysis-ready data needed for the project. A locally hosted interactive web application will display the selected Alberta oil and gas wells using OpenStreetMap, allow users to search or filter wells by company, visually categorize wells according to the project's environmental screening method, and allow users to inspect information about individual wells. Supporting documentation will include data provenance, validation evidence, spatial methodology, baseline and sensitivity-test results, limitations, responsible-use information, and the required course submission materials.

## Feedback and changes

### Current classroom-release scope — 10 November 2026

This dated refinement changes the next-release delivery target in the original scope rows above: preserve real ST37 wells but use synthetic environmental scenarios for a small Red Deer/Drayton showcase. Full official environmental ingestion remains an optional later branch. See [classroom implementation handoff](../docs/classroom_demo.md). The new runtime has not been built by this documentation review.

No real environmental conclusions. |

The full inventory is not deleted or replaced by the display sample. Licensee and licence-status meaning are preserved; fake producer/production values are excluded. Ecological occurrences are not substituted for critical habitat, and simulated ESA is not legal protection. Existing real-water gaps remain documented in the optional real pipeline. Course feedback, final package and D2L statuses still require their own evidence.
