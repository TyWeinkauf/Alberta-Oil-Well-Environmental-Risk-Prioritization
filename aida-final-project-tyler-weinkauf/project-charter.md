# Project charter

- **Project title:** [Working title] - Alberta Oil Well Enviromental Risk Prioritization - Training Project
- **Intended user/team:** [Who could use the work?] - A fictional enviromental reclamation company responsible for reviewing oil and gas well sites in Alberta and prioritizing sites for further enviromental assessment, reclamation planning or additional protective measures.
- **Practical question or decision:** [What will this project help someone understand or decide?] - Which oil and gas well sites within selected Alberta study area should be prioritized for further review based on their proximity to selected enviromental risk or sensitivity features.
- **Project boundary:** [Data period, system, population, place, or records included.] - The project will use permitted public Alberta oil and gas well data and selected public enviromental or geographioc datasets for a defined study area in Alberta. The project will focur on well locations and selected enviromental factors that can be represented using available data and GIS analysis. The exact study are, enviromental factors, data dates and analysis thresholds will be documented as the datasets and project scope are finalized.
- **What is outside scope:** [What the project will not build or claim.] - This project will not provide a professional enviromental risk assessment, determine regulatory compliance, recommend actual field work, or claim that a well is enviromentally unsafe. It will not replace field inspections, engineering assessments, enviromental studies, regulatory review, or consultation with affected communities or indigenious authorities.
- **Shared inputs:** [List inputs used in more than one course, with permission and source.] - Permitted public Alberta oil and gas well location and attribute data will be used as a shared prfoject input where appropriate. Permitted public enviromental and geographic datasets may also be shared between the Data Engineering and GIS components when relevant. The source, license/permission, date/version, fields, and limitations of each dataset will be documented before use.
- **Course contributions:** [Summarize how the separate questions and evidence fit together.] - AIDA 1145 Data Engineering will build a reproducible pipeline to acquire or load, clean, validate, transform, and produce an analysis-ready dataset from the permitted source data. AIDA 2362 GIS will use permitted spatial data to examine the geographic relationship between well locations and selected enviromental sensitivity or risk factors, create a baseline decision map and supporting evidence. The courses share a practical context and may share permitted inputs, but each course will maintain its own question, methods, validation evidence, outputs, and submission requirements.
- **Proposal status:** See the proposal status table in `README.md`.
- **Plan updates:** [Describe how you refined the plan and what changed.] - The approved proposal is being refined into a bounded training project using real, permitted Alberta oil and gas and environmental data where available. The project will use a fictional environmental reclamation company as the intended decision-maker and will clearly state that the resulting prioritization is an educational GIS/data exercise rather than a professional environmental risk assessment. Specific datasets, study area, environmental variables, and analysis thresholds will be finalized after data availability and permission are confirmed.
- **Target finish:** End of Week 15.

## Classroom-release revision — 10 November 2026

The next demonstrable release is a small Red Deer/Drayton Valley training scenario, preserving actual ST37 surface-hole points/attributes and placing explicitly synthetic environmental features around a reproducible regional sample. The complete well inventory and existing ingestion tools remain retained; neither province-wide environmental ingestion nor database credentials on the teacher's laptop are required for the intended classroom runtime. That runtime is still to be implemented.

The user-provided starter is a design reference. Its fully invented wells/production do not replace actual ST37 records, and production remains outside scope. Simulated proximity results demonstrate software and GIS methods; they are not environmental findings or actual prioritization advice about the real licensees/wells. Final bounds/sample settings, coordinate-datum evidence, small-file preparation, seeded features, offline UI/analysis and teacher-package verification are listed in [the classroom handoff](../docs/classroom_demo.md) and DESIGN Phase 7. This refinement records project direction, not new instructor approval or submission evidence.
